## Family notes

METHOD: every file named in the brief was read in full (middleware.ts 317L, app/(platform)/layout.tsx 137L, components/shell/AppShell.tsx 127L + Sidebar 257L + RightRail 182L + Topbar 36L + NavList 115L + MobileDrawer 96L + CommandPalette 445L + ComplianceFooter 97L + AutopilotStickyStop 117L, app/stock/[symbol]/page.tsx 703L, app/settings/page.tsx §55-580, app/(platform)/strategies/page.tsx, app/(platform)/fno/page.tsx, app/providers.tsx, app/layout.tsx, all 12 loading/error files, app/admin/layout.tsx). Shell mounts were determined by `grep -rln "AppShell" app` plus reading each hit's JSX. Redirect targets were checked against the filesystem with `ls app/<target>`. Dead code was determined by a Python import-graph reachability scan (resolves @/ alias, relative specifiers, index barrels and dynamic import()) seeded from every app/ route file + middleware.ts — not by name-grep, which produces false "live" results for generic filenames like terminal.tsx.

THREE HEADLINES:
1. There is no single shell. 18 routes get AppShell from a layout; 13 authed routes mount AppShell INSIDE page.tsx (so their 12 loading.tsx/error.tsx files render bare, with no sidebar, no rails and no compliance footer); /stock/[symbol] — arguably the highest-traffic surface in a stock app — renders a bare <div className="min-h-screen bg-main"> with nothing but its own "Markets › SYMBOL" breadcrumb. The SEBI ComplianceFooter and the SystemHaltBanner kill-switch banner are therefore absent from surfaces the AppShell comment claims they reach.
2. Nine middleware redirect targets 404 because their pages do not exist in this app (app/proof, app/pricing, app/privacy, app/terms, app/models, app/track-record, app/regime, app/legal are all absent — split out to a separate `landing` app per app/page.tsx). /pricing has 22 in-app call sites including the sidebar Upgrade pill and the right-rail "Help & plans" — the two most persistent controls in the product. /legal/* has 4 call sites inside ComplianceFooter, i.e. on EVERY AppShell route.
3. 33 component files are unreachable from any route, including the entire components/managed/ island (5 files) whose SimpleView.tsx is the "Simple view band" the /home and /activity redirects exist to deliver — it is imported by nothing. 21 of the 27 components/ui shadcn primitives are unused.

CORRECTIONS TO THE BRIEF'S CANDIDATE LIST: components/discover/AiTopPicks.tsx is LIVE (app/stocks/page.tsx:56 → :386 `<AiTopPicks limit={12} />`). components/magicui/animated-theme-toggler.tsx is LIVE (components/theme/AnimatedThemeToggle.tsx:16, mounted at the bottom of the 72px RightRail). components/ui/blur-fade.tsx is LIVE (copilot page + ChatArtifacts). Do not delete those three.

REDESIGN IMPLICATION: the shell must become a route-group layout that actually covers the authed surface, otherwise a re-skin will be applied 18 times and missed 14 times. Every /pricing, /proof and /legal/* link is either an external hand-off to the landing app (needs an absolute URL) or a page that must be built here.
