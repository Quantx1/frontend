## Family notes

SHELL SPLIT (important for a redesign): /markets, /stocks and /watchlist all import `AppShell` themselves (there is no route-group layout for these three — they sit at app/ root, not inside app/(platform)). /stock/[symbol] is the exception: it is STANDALONE with `min-h-screen bg-main` + `mx-auto max-w-7xl`, so it has NO left sidebar, NO right rail and NO ComplianceFooter; it substitutes its own mono breadcrumb "Markets › SYMBOL". Any redesign that unifies chrome must decide deliberately whether the terminal joins the shell.

CONTENT WIDTHS: AppShell caps content at max-w-[1440px] with px-4 md:px-6. /markets runs full-bleed inside that cap (`w-full ... p-4 md:p-6 xl:px-8`) — it is a bento dashboard, not a reading column. /stock/[symbol] caps at max-w-7xl (1280px). Both route-level loading.tsx files (stocks, watchlist) use max-w-7xl px-6 py-8, which does NOT match the shell cap — a real inconsistency between the skeleton and the loaded page.

SHARED LAYOUT DNA across /stocks and /watchlist (the "D" list archetype): foundation PageHeader (mono eyebrow → 40px display-sm h1 → description → right-aligned actions) → a body at `space-y-5 px-4 py-5 md:px-6` → a hairline `border-y py-3` filter/toolbar row → a foundation DataTable (rounded-sm hairline frame, sticky bg-wrap-hover header, mono 11px uppercase tracking-0.08em headers, hairline rows, sticky first column, hidden-below-sm columns, and a full stacked-card fallback below sm) → DisclaimerFooter. Both wrap blocks in `Reveal` (framer-motion fade-up y:10→0, 450ms, cubic-bezier .16,1,.3,1) staggered at 0.05s steps.

BOTH ERROR BOUNDARIES ARE THE SAME COMPONENT: app/stocks/error.tsx and app/watchlist/error.tsx are byte-identical apart from class order — an 80px dashed down-red ring spinning over 8s, a bold "!", "Something went wrong", "An unexpected error occurred.", "Try again". /markets and /stock/[symbol] have no route-level error.tsx and fall back to app/error.tsx.

HONEST-EMPTY IS THE HOUSE RULE: nearly every card either renders a named skeleton height or a sentence that names the symbol/feed ("Not enough volume history for RELIANCE yet…", "No institutional flow data yet — check back after the close."). Several render `null` entirely rather than an empty shell: AiRadarStrip, ProbabilityCard, IndicatorInterpreterCard, OrderBookCard (no live feed), SectorRotationCard, WatchlistDigestCard, AiTopPicks, TabAiRead. A redesign must preserve "no card" as a legitimate state — do not substitute placeholder zeros.

COST MODEL VISIBLE IN THE UI: deterministic data loads free; every LLM narrative is either day-cached or explicitly user-triggered. That is why so many cards carry a secondary trigger — "Generate deep read", "Explain this verdict", "AI read", "Ask AI", "What does this mean?", "Run analysis", "Explain" — and a "· cached for today" caption. Those triggers are load-bearing product copy, not decoration.

TWO SEPARATE FOOTNOTE LINES ARE INTENTIONAL on the copilot cards (EntityCard, DataStub): provenance ("Index breadth on settled closes to 2026-08-01.") and the advice statement are never merged into one grey line.

CARDS IN COMPONENTS/STOCK/* NOT RENDERED ON /stock/[symbol]: IndicatorInterpreterCard (superseded by TechnicalsPanelCard — it still exists and compiles but the terminal does not import it) and NewsMoodCard (its aggregate mood was folded into NewsIntelligenceCard; NewsMoodCard is now only reachable through StockMoodLookup on /stocks). Similarly components/markets/MarketTicker.tsx is used by /copilot and HomeCockpit, not by any route in this group. MoversColumns, SectorHeatmap, RegimeGauge, MarketPulseCard, OrderFlowAnalysis, SectorRotationCard, AiRadarStrip, IndexStrip, BreadthCard, BigDealsCard, MarketExplainerCard and DailyBriefingCard all render DIRECTLY on the /markets page as stacked bento cards — there is no slide-over panel on this route.

SEBI / ENTITLEMENT MODEL: raw NSE quotes, FII/DII rupee flows, bulk-block deals, short-selling and L2 depth render only for an entitled viewer (own broker feed or NEXT_PUBLIC_LICENSED_MARKET_DATA=true). Non-entitled users still get a full page built from EOD/derived data, with a per-row "EOD" pill on /stocks and a DataBadge reading "EOD research" everywhere. DisclaimerFooter appears at the bottom of all four screens; AppShell adds ComplianceFooter on the three shelled routes.

MOTION + THEME: only two persistent animations exist by design — the ThinkingLine dot and the marquee tape (not on these routes) — everything else is a one-shot Reveal or a transition. Both themes are first-class: the LightweightChart carries its own hard-coded dark/light palettes (dark bg #151517 / light bg #FFFFFF, up #10B981 vs #0A6B50, down #F5808C vs #B81C22) and is re-keyed and re-mounted when next-themes resolves a flip; every other surface is token-driven and re-skins for free.

## `/markets` — Markets — regime-aware desk

**File** `app/markets/page.tsx` · **307 LOC** · **Access** authed. Data gate: `dataEntitled = useBrokerStatus().isConnected || process.env.NEXT_PUBLIC_LICENSED_MARKET_DATA === 'true'`. When FALSE, IndexStrip is REPLACED in place by the MarketDataGate card (the foreign global-cues chip row survives beneath it, because non-NSE indices are SEBI-safe), MoversColumns disappears entirely (`moversShown` is emptied, and its heading is conditional on the same array), and the whole OrderFlowAnalysis block is unmounted. Everything else — briefing, pulse, radar, breadth, gauge, heatmap, explainer, rotation, headlines, big deals — renders identically for both audiences. No tier gate on this page.

**Shell** — (platform) AppShell — imported directly by the page (app/markets has no route-group layout). Fixed 240px/68px left Sidebar on bg-wrap, fixed 72px RightRail on bg-main, mobile-only Topbar + MobileDrawer below lg, ComplianceFooter appended by AppShell, Cmd/Ctrl+K CommandPalette.

**Purpose.** The complete pre-market research desk, as a full-width bento grid. It is DELIBERATELY a dashboard: an AI briefing hero that reads the session for you, then the visual/interactive layer beneath it — internals, radar, breadth, regime, sectors, movers, rotation, headlines, deals and institutional flow. Ad-hoc questions go to the Copilot dock (⌘/); there is no embedded agent, no follow-up chip row and no slide-over panel on this route.

> **Layout is full-width, not a reading column.** The page container is `w-full space-y-4 p-4 md:p-6 xl:px-8` with 16px vertical rhythm and `lg:grid-cols-12` bento rows. Do not design this as a centred 860px column.
