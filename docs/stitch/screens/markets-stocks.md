## Family notes

SHELL SPLIT (important for a redesign): /markets, /stocks and /watchlist all import `AppShell` themselves (there is no route-group layout for these three — they sit at app/ root, not inside app/(platform)). /stock/[symbol] is the exception: it is STANDALONE with `min-h-screen bg-main` + `mx-auto max-w-7xl`, so it has NO left sidebar, NO right rail and NO ComplianceFooter; it substitutes its own mono breadcrumb "Markets › SYMBOL". Any redesign that unifies chrome must decide deliberately whether the terminal joins the shell.

CONTENT WIDTHS: AppShell caps content at max-w-[1440px] with px-4 md:px-6. /markets deliberately narrows to a 860px reading column. /stock/[symbol] caps at max-w-7xl (1280px). Both route-level loading.tsx files (stocks, watchlist) use max-w-7xl px-6 py-8, which does NOT match the shell cap — a real inconsistency between the skeleton and the loaded page.

SHARED LAYOUT DNA across /stocks and /watchlist (the "D" list archetype): foundation PageHeader (mono eyebrow → 40px display-sm h1 → description → right-aligned actions) → a body at `space-y-5 px-4 py-5 md:px-6` → a hairline `border-y py-3` filter/toolbar row → a foundation DataTable (rounded-sm hairline frame, sticky bg-wrap-hover header, mono 11px uppercase tracking-0.08em headers, hairline rows, sticky first column, hidden-below-sm columns, and a full stacked-card fallback below sm) → DisclaimerFooter. Both wrap blocks in `Reveal` (framer-motion fade-up y:10→0, 450ms, cubic-bezier .16,1,.3,1) staggered at 0.05s steps.

BOTH ERROR BOUNDARIES ARE THE SAME COMPONENT: app/stocks/error.tsx and app/watchlist/error.tsx are byte-identical apart from class order — an 80px dashed down-red ring spinning over 8s, a bold "!", "Something went wrong", "An unexpected error occurred.", "Try again". /markets and /stock/[symbol] have no route-level error.tsx and fall back to app/error.tsx.

HONEST-EMPTY IS THE HOUSE RULE: nearly every card either renders a named skeleton height or a sentence that names the symbol/feed ("Not enough volume history for RELIANCE yet…", "No institutional flow data yet — check back after the close."). Several render `null` entirely rather than an empty shell: AiRadarStrip, ProbabilityCard, IndicatorInterpreterCard, OrderBookCard (no live feed), SectorRotationCard, WatchlistDigestCard, AiTopPicks, TabAiRead. A redesign must preserve "no card" as a legitimate state — do not substitute placeholder zeros.

COST MODEL VISIBLE IN THE UI: deterministic data loads free; every LLM narrative is either day-cached or explicitly user-triggered. That is why so many cards carry a secondary trigger — "Generate deep read", "Explain this verdict", "AI read", "Ask AI", "What does this mean?", "Run analysis", "Explain" — and a "· cached for today" caption. Those triggers are load-bearing product copy, not decoration.

TWO SEPARATE FOOTNOTE LINES ARE INTENTIONAL on the copilot cards (EntityCard, DataStub): provenance ("Index breadth on settled closes to 2026-08-01.") and the advice statement are never merged into one grey line.

## `/markets` — Markets — the twelve-card research desk

**File** `app/markets/page.tsx` · **307 LOC** · **Access** PUBLIC. /markets is listed in PUBLIC_PREFIXES (middleware.ts:85) so the whole desk renders logged-out. A second, orthogonal gate is SEBI Path-A data entitlement: dataEntitled = useBrokerStatus().isConnected || NEXT_PUBLIC_LICENSED_MARKET_DATA === 'true'. Un-entitled viewers lose the IndexStrip (replaced by MarketDataGate), the entire Market movers section, and OrderFlowAnalysis; MarketExplainerCard scrubs NIFTY/VIX quotes from its prose. Every derived surface (briefing, pulse, radar, breadth, regime, heatmap, rotation, headlines, big deals) stays visible to everyone.

**Shell** — <AppShell> — 3-zone chrome. Fixed left sidebar 240px expanded / 68px collapsed (bg-wrap, preference persisted at localStorage 'quantx.sidebar.collapsed.v2', 200ms margin transition once mounted); fixed 72px right icon rail (desktop only); main pane between them, mx-auto max-w-[1440px] px-4 md:px-6, with ConnectBrokerBanner above the page and ComplianceFooter below it. Skip-link 'Skip to main content' is the first focusable node. Cmd/Ctrl+K opens CommandPalette; Escape closes palette and mobile drawer. Below lg the rails vanish and the mobile Topbar + MobileDrawer take over. Prose font inside the shell is Plus Jakarta Sans via appSans; numerics via appMono.

**Purpose.** The pre-market research hub and post-close wrap: one scrolling desk that answers 'what is the market doing and why' from an AI briefing hero down through internals, radar, regime, sectors, movers, rotation, headlines, big deals and institutional order-flow. Ad-hoc questions are pushed to the Copilot dock (Cmd//) — there is no embedded agent, no slide-over and no tabbed panel on this page.

Tier gates live inside cards: AIDossierPanel hides all meters/scores for tier 'free' and shows a Pro upgrade strip; its Counterpoint debate hook is Elite-only; ChartVisionCard is hidden for free upstream, symbol-restricted on Pro (403 vision_symbol_restricted → an upgrade link) and unrestricted on Elite via anywhere=true (an "Elite" pill renders in its header).

**Shell** — STANDALONE, full-bleed — this route does NOT use AppShell. It renders `min-h-screen bg-main` with an inner `mx-auto max-w-7xl px-4 py-4 md:px-6 md:py-6`, so there is no left sidebar, no right rail and no ComplianceFooter; only the root layout (Geist fonts, next-themes tri-theme, Providers with SWRConfig, AuthProvider, sonner Toaster, GlobalCopilot dock) wraps it. The page provides its own breadcrumb instead of the shell chrome.

**Purpose.** The Bloomberg-style terminal for one NSE symbol. The user comes to see what price is doing, what every engine reads into it, why it moved and what the base rates say — then acts (trade ticket, watchlist, Copilot). It concludes with one deep-reasoning PM-grade synthesis at the top and the deterministic evidence for that synthesis grouped under three tabs beneath it.

**States & data.** Page-level: api.screener.getStockPrice(symbol) + api.screener.getTechnicals(symbol) fetched together on mount and re-polled every 30s while the document is visible; usePriceUpdates([symbol]) applies live WebSocket ticks (ltp / change / change_percentage) on top; api.watchlist.getAll() checks membership. Two shared SWR keys: `fundamentals:{symbol}` (300s dedupe, feeds BOTH the header Mkt Cap/P-E and FundamentalsCard) and `signals:today` (60s refresh, feeds the header AI Picks badge). Every AI/data card is a dynamic import with ssr:false, so it hydrates only when its tab or module mounts, and each fetches its own key: deep-read:{sym}, verdict:{sym}, dossier:{sym}, tech-panel:{sym}, sent-read:{sym}, newsintel:{sym}, relativeStrength, volumeIntel + footprint, probability, technicalPanel, patternsV2Explain (chart overlay) and getStockHistory (chart + volume profile). LLM narratives are all user-triggered or day-cached: Trade Desk generates on click, TabAiRead loads per-tab, Why Moving / Earnings preview fire only on their button. OrderBookCard polls every 5s and returns null without a broker feed. Every card has an explicit skeleton height and an honest-empty sentence naming the symbol; several (ProbabilityCard, OrderBookCard, TabAiRead, AiRadar-style panels) render null rather than an empty shell. Errors surface as sonner toasts ("Couldn't refresh {SYMBOL}", "Could not update watchlist") and per-card ErrorBoundaries.

**Interactions.** Breadcrumb "Markets" routes to /markets. Watchlist button optimistically toggles with rollback + toast. "Ask Copilot" dispatches the global Copilot dock with a prefilled question. Refresh re-runs the price/technicals fetch (icon spins). Trade opens QuickTrade then a mandatory ConfirmDialog before any broker order. The chart is a full terminal: 9 range pills, 3 interval pills, 3 chart types, three EMA toggles, a Log toggle, a Pattern-overlay toggle, crosshair-driven OHLC legend, pan/zoom, price-line axis labels. Three AI tabs switch panes (Radix Tabs, keyboard arrow navigation, horizontally scrollable rail). Per-card triggers: "Generate deep read", "Ask Copilot about this read", "Explain this verdict", "AI read", "Run analysis"/"Re-run", "Ask AI"/"Refresh" (Why moving, Earnings), "Explain", "What does this mean?". InfoDot popovers in FundamentalsCard open on click and close on blur. Story headlines and signal chips open external links / /signals/{id}. Theme flips re-key and re-mount the chart.

**Responsive.** Container is max-w-7xl with px-4 py-4 → md:px-6 md:py-6. Header band is a column below lg and a row at lg+; the identity/price rows wrap. Quick-stat dl is 2 cols → sm:3 → lg:4.
