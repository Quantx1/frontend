## `/autopilot` — AutoPilot Cockpit

**File** `app/(platform)/autopilot/page.tsx` · **1064 LOC** · **Access** authed · everyone can run paper (free); `mode==='live'` needs Pro/Elite + `broker_connected` + a one-time suitability quiz (403 detail.code `suitability_quiz_required` → router.push('/onboarding/risk-quiz')). HTTP 402 is handled by the platform error boundary → UpgradeModal.

**Shell** — (platform) AppShell (240/68px sidebar, 72px right rail, 1440px cap) + mobile-only AutopilotStickyStop FAB

**Purpose.** The control room for the supervised auto-trading engine. The user comes here to see whether the bot is on, in practice or live mode, what regime/VIX exposure it is running, to set the three safety rails, to read every rebalance tick and trade it fired, and — above all — to stop it. It concludes: 'here is exactly what my money robot did today, and here are the two buttons that halt it.'
