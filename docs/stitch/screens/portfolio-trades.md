## Family notes

SHARED SHELL — /portfolio, /trades and /trades/[id] live OUTSIDE the (platform) route group and each page imports and renders <AppShell> itself (trades even renders it inside its loading branch). /paper-trading lives INSIDE app/(platform)/ and is wrapped by PlatformLayout, which adds a dynamically-imported SystemHaltBanner above AppShell and an AutopilotStickyStop below, and which redirects to /onboarding/broker-connect when onboarding is incomplete. AppShell itself: fixed 240px left sidebar (68px collapsed, persisted under localStorage key 'quantx.sidebar.collapsed.v2', margin transition 200ms), fixed 72px right icon rail from lg, main pane with lg:ml-60 / lg:ml-[68px] and lg:mr-[72px], a sr-only skip-to-content link, ConnectBrokerBanner at the top of main, content capped at max-w-[1440px] with px-4 md:px-6, and ComplianceFooter appended at the bottom of EVERY authed surface. Cmd/Ctrl+K toggles the CommandPalette; Escape closes palette and MobileDrawer; the drawer auto-closes on route change. All four routes therefore double-pad horizontally (shell gutter + page padding).

GUTTER PATTERN — /portfolio and /trades both use `w-full space-y-5 p-4 md:p-6 xl:px-8`; /paper-trading uses `px-4 md:px-6 py-6 w-full space-y-5`; /trades/[id] is the outlier with a narrow centred reading column `mx-auto max-w-5xl space-y-6 px-6 py-8`. 20px is the standard vertical rhythm on three of the four.

CARD SURFACES ARE NOT GLASS — the `.glass-*` class names are historical. `.lg-surface / .glass-surface / .glass-card` now resolve to opaque bg-wrap + 1px line + elev-1; `.glass-control` is an opaque L2 fill with a hairline; `.glass-control-accent` is a solid #406AE4 fill with white ink; `.glass-control-danger` is a down-tinted fill with a down/32% border. Real translucency survives only on `.glass-float`/`.glass-pill` (palette, sheets, dropdowns). Card variant="glass" maps to elevation 'floating' (shadow-elev-2), which is why the /portfolio Performance and Positions cards read heavier than the flat panels elsewhere. The page canvas paints one static radial accent pool at 6% (dark) / 3% (light) anchored top-left — no animated mesh.

TWO CARD IDIOMS COEXIST — the foundation `Card` (rounded-md 12px, 20/16 padding, CardHeader at 16px/600 with a min-h-44px bordered row) is used on /portfolio and /trades/[id]; a hand-rolled `CARD = 'rounded-sm border border-line bg-wrap p-4'` constant with a font-mono 10–11px uppercase tracking-[0.1em] eyebrow instead of a CardHeader is used on /paper-trading and inside the journal/paper components. Radii across the group are inconsistent by design of history: 8px (paper panels, journal cards, KPI StatCards), 12px (foundation Card), 16px (AchievementsStrip, PaperWindowCard, PaperLeagueLeaderboard, and the two /portfolio glass cards which force rounded-lg).

NUMERIC TREATMENT — every money/percent value uses the MONO helper ('[font-family:var(--font-mono)] tabular-nums'). Rupee amounts go through lib/format's Indian 2-2-3 grouping (num / numMax / inr), null renders as an em dash '—' and never as '-' or '--'. Signs are explicit ('+₹4,120'), percents are 2dp except the Win Rate KPI (1dp) and the achievement/return strips (2dp). Colour is applied by the component, never by the formatter, and up/down tints are reserved strictly for P&L.

TABLES — three different table implementations appear in this group and a redesign must not unify them blindly: (1) the foundation DataTable (used on /portfolio and in BrokerPositionsPanel) with a sticky bg-wrap-hover header of font-mono 11px uppercase tracking-[0.08em] labels, three-click sort cycling asc→desc→cleared, aria-sort, sticky first column, hideOnMobile columns, and an automatic below-sm collapse into stacked label/value cards; (2) the hand-built 12-column grid on /trades with entirely separate desktop and mobile markup and a proportional P&L bar; (3) the plain <table> in PaperLeagueLeaderboard with its own overflow-x-auto.

BADGE TONES — Badge supports primary / up / down / warning / muted / ai / buy / hold / sell, all rendered as rounded-full 12px/500 pills with a 10% tint fill and a 20% border. This group uses: buy/sell for LONG/SHORT and BUY/SELL, warning for the pending-approval count, and up/down/warning for broker order status (COMPLETE/FILLED → up, REJECTED/CANCELLED → down, everything else → warning).

HONEST-EMPTY IS A HARD RULE — several components render NOTHING rather than a placeholder: TradePatternsCard below 5 closed trades, TradeReviewCard when the analysis endpoint returns no points, RiskBanner when there are no warnings OR when the check fails, BrokerPositionsPanel while broker status is still loading. Comments state explicitly that a failed positions query must not be conflated with an empty book — the /portfolio KPI tiles were removed for exactly that reason and replaced by the RenderedSurface sentence. Any redesign that reintroduces a "₹0 · 0 positions" tile row would reverse a deliberate decision.

AI COST MODEL IS VISIBLE IN THE UI — deterministic reads load automatically and cost zero tokens; every LLM path is a user-triggered button labelled distinctly ('Discover', 'Coach me', 'Get AI review', 'Regenerate', 'Ask AI about this plan', 'Ask AI'). Those buttons are uniformly 10–11px accent-ink text buttons with a Sparkles icon that swaps to a spinning Loader2 and disables while busy. RenderedSurface on /portfolio POSTs /api/ai/copilot/render, which is explicitly outside the chat credit cap.

MOTION — Reveal (framer-motion, opacity 0→1 + y 10→0, 450ms, cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1)) wraps almost every top-level block, with stagger delays of 0.03–0.20s. Two bespoke CSS animations exist only on /trades: 'trade-row-in' (row slide-in, 0.35s, staggered index×0.04s) and the empty-state SVG line-draw + plus-glyph fade. Button presses scale to 0.98 at 90ms.

BROKER GATING — useBrokerStatus (SWR on /api/broker/connections, 30s dedupe) is the single source of truth. BrokerLock has two modes: the plain card used on /portfolio (44px lock circle, '{feature} needs a connected broker', a description, and an accent 'Connect your broker' link to /settings?tab=broker) and a frosted mode that blurs a decorative preview behind a lock overlay (the preview is deliberately shape-only, never fabricated prices). Paper trading is intentionally ungated — QuickTrade's paperMode skips BrokerLock entirely.

COMPLIANCE — DisclaimerFooter ('For educational purposes. Not investment advice. Markets carry risk.', 10px centred muted) sits at the bottom of /portfolio, /trades and /trades/[id]; /paper-trading substitutes its own longer slippage footnote. The statutory ComplianceFooter is appended by AppShell to all four and carries deliberate '[pending]' / '[to be confirmed]' placeholders that must not be replaced with invented registration numbers.

## `/portfolio` — Portfolio (book + live broker)

**File** `app/portfolio/page.tsx` · **386 LOC** · **Access** authed (middleware redirects to /login without a Supabase cookie). No tier gate. The Broker section is broker-gated: BrokerPositionsPanel renders <BrokerLock> until a broker connection with status 'connected' exists; the platform positions table + chart above it are ungated.

## `/trades` — Trade History (journal + closed trades)

**File** `app/trades/page.tsx` · **625 LOC** · **Access** authed (middleware cookie gate). No tier gate on the page. Two sub-cards self-gate on data: TradePatternsCard returns null below 5 closed trades; WeeklyReviewCard's 'Regenerate' path is the Pro+ on-demand LLM route (the card itself reads the free cron-generated row). The 'Pending Approval' block only exists in semi-auto mode, i.e. when GET /api/trades?status=pending returns rows.

**Shell** — Route sits OUTSIDE the (platform) group — root layout only; the page renders <AppShell> itself (including in its loading branch). Same 240/68px sidebar + 72px right rail + max-w-[1440px] px-4 md:px-6 main pane, ConnectBrokerBanner on top, ComplianceFooter at the bottom.

**Purpose.** The trader's journal of executed and closed trades. They come here to review what actually happened — total P&L, win rate, win/loss split — to approve or reject semi-auto trades still waiting on them, to filter wins vs losses, to export the record as CSV, and to open an inline AI post-mortem on any single trade. Two journal cards at the top conclude the behavioural read: this week vs NIFTY, and the patterns mined from their own closed trades.
